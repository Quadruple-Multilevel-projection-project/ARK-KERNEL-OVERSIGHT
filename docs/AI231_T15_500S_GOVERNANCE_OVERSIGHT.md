# ARK Kernel Oversight — T15 and 500-Second Governance Controls

Status: proposed oversight requirements; implementation and certification are not claimed.

## Scope
Integrate T15 (obligations, contracts, debt-service alerts, cost externalization, remedies and public review), the 500-Second Sovereignty Test (supply disruption, platform dependency, public-budget shock and trust breakdown), and citizen services (proposals, evidence, deliberation, consultations, correction and appeal).

## Mandatory controls
1. Every case and simulation has a unique ID, declared scope, source inventory, assumptions, and version.
2. Separate observations, source claims, derived assessments, hypotheses, and recommendations.
3. Preserve provenance and replay inputs; a digest proves integrity of a captured artifact, not its truth.
4. Require human review before any consequential recommendation is treated as an institutional position.
5. An institution that is party to a dispute cannot be its sole reviewer.
6. Provide rights-impact review, privacy, accessibility, anti-coercion safeguards, role separation, appeal, and stop.
7. Civic participation cannot be weighted by token ownership, payment, or economic contribution.
8. Simulated consultations are never represented as official elections.
9. A 500-second exercise is a simulation window, not a scientifically validated crisis threshold.
10. T15 is an alert/accountability workflow, not a guarantee that a complex inquiry finishes in fifteen seconds.

## State model
INTAKE → SOURCE_CHECK → ANALYSIS → HUMAN_REVIEW → PUBLIC_RESPONSE / REMEDY_OPTIONS → APPEAL → CLOSED.
At any stage: ABSTAIN, PAUSE, or ESCALATE when evidence, authority, or safeguards are insufficient.

## Acceptance evidence
Replayable case bundle; independent reviewer record; rights/privacy checklist; citizen appeal trace; scenario assumptions and output report; test showing payment cannot alter civic participation; release manifest and change log.
